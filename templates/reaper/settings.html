WHAT WE STORE

Your GitHub profile, encrypted GitHub and OpenAI credentials, repository metadata, evidence inventory, AI results, scan history, and queue preferences. API caches are encrypted and expire.

WHAT THE MODEL SEES

A limited sample of metadata, manifests, docs, and source excerpts. Requests disable response storage, but provider abuse-monitoring retention may still apply. Source excerpts are not retained in scan records.

WHAT STAYS YOURS

No public profiles or publishing routes exist. No analysis is public, including analyses of public repositories.

WHEN YOU DELETE

Live records are removed immediately. An in-flight provider request cannot be recalled. Backups expire under the operator’s retention policy; provider retention is separate.
